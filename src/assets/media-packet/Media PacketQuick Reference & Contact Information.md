About Our Center

Mommy Angel's Specialty Care is a premier ABA therapy and early childhood development facility with over 10 years of experience. We provide a deeply nurturing environment that bridges expert clinical intervention with traditional childhood play for children with autism and developmental delays.

**Our Mission**  
To operate as a true Development Partner for the families we serve, offering empathy-driven, evidence-based ABA therapy to empower children to thrive, build essential life skills, and experience the warmth of an inclusive, social setting.

**Our Philosophy**  
*"Socialization, not isolation."* We believe in comprehensive care by treating the whole child, combining BCBA-led therapy with the enriching experiences of our traditional daycare for inclusive peer interaction.Comprehensive ServicesCore Therapeutic Interventions

* **Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) Therapy:** BCBA-supervised, evidence-based treatment.  
    
* **Speech & Language Therapy:** Communication skill development and articulation.  
* **Occupational Therapy:** Sensory integration and fine/gross motor skills.  
* **1:1 Individualized Therapy:** Deeply personalized care tailored to unique developmental milestones.

Educational & Social Programs

* Pre-K Readiness Program  
* Socialization Programs (Facilitated peer interaction)  
* Multilingual Instruction Capabilities  
* Life Skills Training

Diagnostics & Assessment

* Progress Monitoring (Data-driven treatment adjustments)
